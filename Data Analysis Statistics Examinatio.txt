*Q7. Covariance*
X: 1,2,3,4. Y: 20,40,60,80
- *(a)* Mean X = *2.5*, Mean Y = *50*
- *(b)* Table:
  X-dev: -1.5, -0.5, 0.5, 1.5
  Y-dev: -30, -10, 10, 30
  Product: 45, 5, 5, 45. Sum = *100*
- *(c)* Sample Cov = 100 / (4-1) = *33.3333*
